    Roadmap to the platinum

    Play in this order. Everything below is built from the official trophy descriptions — chapter and mission names, collectible counts, and the 100% requirement — not from guesswork. Where the official description does not state a detail, the guide says so.

    1. Stage 1 — Story first (Chapters 1–4 + finale)

      Earn the chapter trophies Paradise Lost, T**s Ass Drug Money, Vice City, Baby! and Top Of The World, Ma, the mission trophies (Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) and the gold You Gotta Know When to Get Off for the final mission. Keep a separate save before the finale and before any big story choice: three trophies look tied to one-off choices or moments (see Missables).

    2. Stage 2 — Contact strands

      Finish the final mission for each named contact: Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone — Road to Rehabilitation, This Was No Boat Accident, The Lone Rider, Supply & Demand, Spin On This!. Along the way, complete 8 stranger strands for Strangers & Freaks.

    3. Stage 3 — See all of Leonida

      Explore the whole map for The Goodtime State, discover 100 vehicles (Whip It Good), WAiNK It at 100 locations (You WAiNKer!), meditate at every spot (Positive Mental Attitude) and register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app (On the Spot). Do these while travelling between missions — they stack with normal play.

    4. Stage 4 — Activities & skills

      Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges (The Shootist), complete 250 stunts (Cunning Stunts), win 20 different races (Earn Cash, Not Kudos) and a drone race at every location (Top Fun), beat Cal at fishing (Baited My Tackle), finish an arcade game (Stick Fiddler), bowl at Kolt & Associates (Let’s Go Bowling, Ten Pin) and try every activity once (Master of None).

    5. Stage 5 — Crime, money & collections

      Rob 5 bank vaults (Maximum Withdrawal), an armored truck (A Classic Blitz Play), a jewelry store undetected (Sneakthief) and an illegal operation per gang (Pest Control); launder $250,000 in Marked Bills (Dime on the Dollar), spend $100,000 (Treat Yo Self), buy all garage properties (Little Boxes) and complete 3 card sets (Don’t Touch My Deck).

    6. Stage 6 — Clean-up & 100%

      Complete 50 Challenges (One Last Thing), hunt Bret's unique animal (Fair Game), then chase A Long Road — 100% completion — and the platinum Vice Got a Price! unlocks with it.

    Missable trophies

    PlayStation does not mark any trophy in the official list as missable, and this guide does not invent flags. Three trophies are flagged possibly missable because their official descriptions describe a single story choice or a one-off moment — do them when they come up, and keep a separate save first. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free roam, as far as the official descriptions state.
